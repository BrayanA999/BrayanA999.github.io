Easy Recipe: Chicken & Rice Bowl

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of cooked rice
  • 1 chicken breast
  • Mixed vegetables
  • Olive oil
  • Your favorite seasonings

Instructions

  1. Cook the rice according to the package.
  2. Season and cook the chicken thoroughly.
  3. Cook the vegetables until tender.
  4. Place everything into a bowl and enjoy.

Preparation Time: About 25 minutes

Estimated Cost: $3.50 per serving

Tips for Saving Money on Meals

  • Plan your meals before grocery shopping.
  • Buy ingredients in larger quantities when practical.
  • Use affordable foods like rice, beans, and eggs.
  • Prepare meals ahead of time.
  • Look for grocery store sales and discounts.

Why Healthy Eating Matters

College life can be busy and expensive. Choosing affordable, nutritious meals can help students maintain their energy, stay focused in class, and build healthier everyday habits.
