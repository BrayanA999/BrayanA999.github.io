Budget Breakdown

This pie chart shows common monthly expenses: savings, utilities, transportation, housing, and food. Savings is the most important category to keep in check because the interest you earn builds your long-term and retirement funds. Food takes up the smallest percentage because it is easy to make a healthy breakfast on a budget—and this guide will walk you through how to do it.

Top 4 Affordable Breakfast Staple Foods

Oats

High in fiber and one of the most versatile breakfast staples.

$0.20 / serving

Eggs

Packed with high-quality protein and essential nutrients.

$0.25 / egg

Bananas

Rich in potassium and other nutrients; pairs great with oats or smoothies.

$0.19 / banana

Lentils

An affordable protein source loaded with fiber and essential vitamins.

$0.30 / serving

Budget Shopping List

Weekly Essentials: Oats, Eggs, Bananas, Peanut Butter

Quick Dishes: Yogurt, Tuna, Beef Jerky, Brussels Sprouts

Overall College Budgeting Tips

  • Buy used books or rent them digitally
  • Use student public transportation instead of paying for rideshare services like Uber
  • Use your campus resources and amenities to your advantage
  • Cook meals at home instead of eating out
